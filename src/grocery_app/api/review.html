Grocery receipts, item by item

This app reads grocery receipts and turns them into a history of what you buy: where the money goes, how often you buy things, and how their prices change.

This is a demo, and the app is still being built. The receipts in it are made up. Look around, answer its questions, or scan a receipt of your own with the + button. Everything you add is wiped when the demo restarts, so please leave out receipts with personal details.
