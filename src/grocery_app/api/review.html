Grocery receipts, item by item

This app reads grocery receipts and turns them into a history of what you buy: where the money goes, how often you buy things, and how their prices change.

This is a demo, and the app is still being built. It is the real app with six months of made-up receipts in it: look around, open a month, a price, a receipt, and answer its questions. What you change is undone when the demo restarts.
